Secrets Baby Beach sits on the quiet southeastern tip of Aruba — past the town of San Nicolas, tucked into a bay where the water runs clear and genuinely calm. This is not the high-rise, cruise-ship end of the island. It's the end where the water is still enough to see your feet at six feet deep.

The resort is adults-only and all-inclusive: seven dining venues (South American fusion at Tierra is the one worth dressing up for), three pools including an oceanfront infinity pool, and a spa that does proper couples treatments. Everything from meals and top-shelf drinks to non-motorized water sports and nightly entertainment is covered.

What's Included in Your Stay

Secrets Baby Beach runs on an Unlimited-Luxury® model — no wristbands, no tiered drink tickets, no surprise charges at checkout for things that should just be included. Here's what's covered from the moment you arrive:

All meals, all day — 7 dining venues including Tierra (South American fusion), Oceana (fresh seafood), Portofino (Italian), and Himitsu (Pan-Asian)
Top-shelf spirits, house wines, and specialty cocktails at all bars — no upgrade required
24-hour in-suite dining — order whenever, delivered to your room
Three pools: social pool with daytime activities, adults-only infinity pool overlooking the bay, and a Preferred Club pool
Non-motorized water sports — snorkeling gear, kayaks, paddleboards
Daily yoga by the infinity pool and fitness classes in the ocean-view gym
Evening entertainment and themed events nightly
Bike tours to San Nicolas — the mural district is a ten-minute ride

Baby Beach itself is a short shuttle ride from the resort. Bring snorkeling fins if you have them — the channel running through the bay has sea turtles and bright reef fish, and the current is stronger than it looks.
