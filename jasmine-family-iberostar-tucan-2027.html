The Annual Trip, Round Two
Back to Where the Jungle Meets the Caribbean

Iberostar Waves Tucán sits on one of the best stretches of beach in the Riviera Maya — wide white sand, calm turquoise water, and a palm canopy thick enough to keep things cool even at midday. The resort is built into the jungle rather than on top of it, so you get both a proper Caribbean beach day and the feeling of waking up surrounded by tropical forest. It's all-inclusive, so meals, drinks, and most activities are covered from the moment you check in — easy for a group this size to spread out and still end up at the same table for dinner.

About the Resort
🍽 Food & Drink
Multiple restaurants ranging from casual buffet to specialty à la carte — Mexican, Italian, and international. All meals and premium bar service included around the clock.
🏖 The Beach
Direct access to a long, calm stretch of Caribbean beach with palapa loungers, non-motorized water sports, and clear, shallow water — excellent for swimming.
🏊 Pools & Grounds
Sprawling free-form pool complex winding through the property, surrounded by mature palms and thatched palapas. The grounds feel genuinely lush — not manicured.
🤿 Activities & Extras
Snorkeling, kayaking, beach volleyball, daytime entertainment, nightly shows, spa, fitness center, and a dive center on site for certified divers.
Local city tax note: A separate tax of MXN 58.66 per room, per night is required by the municipality and collected directly by the resort at check-in. This is not included in your quoted price and is paid on-site.
