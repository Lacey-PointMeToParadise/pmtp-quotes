Union Station is Nashville's most architecturally dramatic hotel — a restored 1900 Romanesque railway station with soaring stained-glass ceilings, 20-foot vaulted room ceilings, and the kind of lobby you walk into and stop mid-sentence. It's a member of Historic Hotels of America and part of Marriott's Autograph Collection. On-site you have Stationairy restaurant, The Bar Car with handcrafted Tennessee cocktails, and a fitness center. The birthday dinner setting here is genuinely theatrical.
