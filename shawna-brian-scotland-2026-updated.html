Budget a Little Extra For

Local taxis between hotels and train stations — budget roughly $100–150 for the full trip
Oyster Card for the London Tube — pick one up at Heathrow when you land and load ~£20–30 per person. It's the easiest way to get around the city and far cheaper than individual tickets.
Gratuities for tour guides — £5–10 per person per tour is customary in Scotland
Eilean Donan Castle interior (Isle of Skye tour) — ~$9.50/person, paid in cash on the day. The exterior visit is included; going inside is optional but worth it.
Stirling Castle admission (Kelpies & Loch Lomond tour) — ~$26/person, paid on the day. The tour stops here; entry is your call.
