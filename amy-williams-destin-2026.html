Hotel Effie is the only full-service luxury hotel inside the gates of Sandestin Golf and Beach Resort — and nothing else along this stretch of the Florida Panhandle comes close. The building itself is striking: a grand white structure with a rooftop pool that's the only one of its kind in all of Northwest Florida. Inside, 250 rooms and suites are finished with the kind of detail that makes you notice it — real wood, proper lighting, walk-in showers that actually deliver pressure.

Your Junior Suite with Resort View comes with a king bed, a private balcony overlooking the resort, and a bathroom big enough to be a destination on its own. The beach is a short tram ride away, and when you get there, you're met with Hotel Effie's signature navy lounge chairs and attendants — no hunting for a spot, no hauling gear. Breakfast at ARA, the hotel's ground-floor restaurant, is the kind of meal that anchors a morning. And when evening comes, the rooftop is where you'll want to be.

The Emerald Coast at Its Best

Sugar-white sand. Turquoise water. Warm August nights on a rooftop with a view that stretches to the horizon.

At the Resort
🏊
Rooftop Pool & ARA Bar
The social heartbeat of Hotel Effie. Heated pool, lounge chairs, white cabanas, cocktails, and live music on weekends. Nothing else like it in the area.
💆
Spa Lilliana
An elevator ride from your room. Swedish massage, hot stone, couples' treatments, facials. August books out fast — I can arrange this in advance.
🏖️
Private Beach Day
Tram to the beach, chairs pre-set with the Hotel Effie logo, attendants for towels and drinks. The Emerald Coast water is some of the clearest on the Gulf.
⛵
Marina Rentals
Kayaks, paddleboards, pontoon boats, and bicycles available at the Sandestin marina. A few hours on the bay is a completely different vibe from the beach.
⛳
Golf (4 Courses)
Raven, Burnt Pine, Baytowne, and The Links — all championship courses, all available to Hotel Effie guests. Raven is the one worth waking up early for.
🎶
Village of Baytowne Wharf
A 5-minute walk from the hotel. Waterfront dining, live music, shops, zip line, and a climbing wall. Good for an easy dinner or a festive night out.
Around Destin
  • 🎣
    ~15 min by car
    Destin Harbor Boardwalk
    The hub of Destin's seafood scene. Deep-sea fishing charters leave from here every morning, and when the boats come back, the dockside restaurants turn those catches into dinner. AJ's on the harbor has been the go-to for two decades. Dolphin and sunset cruises run evenings.
  • 🌿
    ~10 min by car
    Henderson Beach State Park
    A rare undeveloped stretch of Panhandle coastline — no umbrellas for sale, no music, just 6,000 feet of protected white sand and emerald water. The dunes here are the real thing. Go early, bring water, and plan to stay a few hours.
  • 🌊
    ~20 min east
    30A & Rosemary Beach
    The string of curated beach towns along Scenic 30A — Rosemary Beach, Seaside, Watercolor, and Alys Beach — are worth a day trip. Good coffee, serious restaurants, and architecture that actually has a point of view. Bud & Alley's at Seaside for lunch.
  • 🛍️
    ~10 min by car
    Silver Sands Premium Outlets
    Florida's largest outlet mall, with over 100 stores. Coach, Kate Spade, J.Crew, Lululemon, and more. A solid option for a rain morning or a late checkout afternoon before heading to the airport.
  • 🤿
    ~15 min by car
    Snorkeling at the East Jetty
    One of the most accessible snorkel spots in the area. The East Pass jetty creates calm, clear water where fish, crabs, and the occasional sea turtle congregate. No gear rental needed — you can pick up a mask at CVS and be in the water within the hour.
