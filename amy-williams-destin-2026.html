Your Hotel

Hotel Effie Sandestin
Autograph Collection · Miramar Beach, Destin  ·  #1 of 18 hotels in the area
Junior Suite · King Resort View Aug 20–23 · 3 Nights Cancel by Aug 17

Hotel Effie is the only full-service luxury hotel inside Sandestin Golf and Beach Resort — and it shows. The rooftop pool is the only one of its kind in Northwest Florida, the spa is properly resort-caliber, and the rooms are finished at a level you don't usually find along this stretch of the Panhandle. Your Junior Suite comes with a king bed, resort views from a private balcony, walk-in shower, and enough space to actually settle in for the weekend. The beach is a short tram ride away — those navy chairs with the Hotel Effie logo you see on the white sand? Those are for guests. So are the chairs, umbrellas, and towels.

At the Resort
Rooftop Pool & Bar
Heated rooftop pool with cabanas, cocktails, and Gulf views — the social center of the hotel. Evenings here have live music most weekends.
Spa Lilliana
Full-service spa with massages, facials, and body treatments. Book ahead — it fills up quickly in August, especially weekends.
Beach Access
Complimentary tram drops you at the beach with chairs and umbrellas waiting. The Emerald Coast sand here is the powdery white sugar kind.
Village of Baytowne Wharf
Steps from the hotel — a waterfront village with restaurants, shops, live entertainment, zip-lining, and a climbing wall. Good for a casual evening out.
Golf (4 Courses)
Sandestin has four championship courses — Raven, Burnt Pine, Baytowne, and The Links. All available to Hotel Effie guests.
Marina Rentals
Kayaks, paddleboards, bicycles, and pontoon boats available at the Sandestin marina. Easy to spend a few hours out on the bay.
Around Destin
  • 🎣
    Destin Harbor Boardwalk — About 15 min by car. Deep-sea fishing charters, waterfront restaurants, dolphin cruises, and some of the best seafood on the Gulf. Emerald Grande overlooks the whole harbor.
  • 🏖️
    Henderson Beach State Park — A rare undeveloped stretch of coastline just minutes away. No crowds, no umbrellas for sale — just 6,000 feet of protected white sand and emerald water.
  • 🛍️
    Silver Sands Premium Outlets — Florida's largest outlet mall, about 10 min from Sandestin. Over 100 stores including Coach, Kate Spade, and J.Crew. Good for a rainy afternoon.
  • 🌊
    30A & Rosemary Beach — About 20 min east. The curated, walkable beach towns along Scenic 30A — Rosemary Beach, Seaside, and Alys Beach — are worth a day trip. Good coffee, great food, beautiful architecture.
  • 🦈
    Snorkeling at Destin Jetties — The East Jetty is one of the most accessible snorkel spots in the area. Calm, clear water with lots of marine life right at the surface.
